# LEGO Business Research & Data Analytics
An independent data analytics portfolio project exploring LEGO's
business, manufacturing, supply chain, and operations.

## Project Objectives

This project aims to:

- Understand LEGO's global business and manufacturing operations
- Study LEGO's manufacturing and supply chain processes
- Research LEGO Manufacturing Vietnam
- Identify potential business and operational KPIs
- Explore how data analytics can support manufacturing operations
- Build analytical datasets using SQL and Python
- Develop a Power BI dashboard based on the research

## Research Areas

### 1. LEGO Business
- Company overview
- Global markets
- Business model
- Revenue and growth

### 2. Manufacturing
- Global manufacturing locations
- Manufacturing process
- Production capacity
- Quality control

### 3. Supply Chain
- Raw materials
- Suppliers
- Inventory
- Warehousing
- Distribution

### 4. LEGO Manufacturing Vietnam
- Factory overview
- Manufacturing capabilities
- Sustainability
- Supply chain role
- Target markets

### 5. Data & Analytics
- Data sources
- Data model
- SQL analysis
- KPI development
- Power BI dashboard
